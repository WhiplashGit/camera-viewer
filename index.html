Setup

Groups

Each group is one grid. "All cameras" always shows every camera that isn't hidden.

Display

Motion pop-ups

The page checks every camera and pops one up only when a person, vehicle or animal moves. Grass, leaves, shadows and lighting changes are ignored. Starred key cameras take over the full screen with a sound. This runs only while the page is open and changes nothing on the server.

See what the AI sees

Pick a camera, stand or walk in view, and press Check. Every object the AI notices is boxed with how sure it is. Pop-ups need about 45% or more on Normal.
