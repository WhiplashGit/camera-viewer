Setup

Groups

Each group is one grid. "All cameras" always shows every camera that isn't hidden.

Display

Motion pop-ups

The page checks every camera and pops one up only when a person, vehicle or animal moves. Grass, leaves, shadows and lighting changes are ignored. Starred key cameras take over the full screen with a sound. This runs only while the page is open and changes nothing on the server.

Your sounds

Upload your own alert sounds (MP3, M4A or WAV, up to 3 MB), then pick one for any camera in the list below. Uploaded sounds are kept in this browser, so add them on each device you use.

See what the AI sees

Pick a camera, stand or walk in view, and press Check. Every object the AI notices is boxed with how sure it is. Pop-ups need about 45% or more on Normal.

Cameras

Pick a sound for each camera, rename it, hide it, star it as a key camera (front gate, front door) for the bigger alert, or untick Motion to never get pop-ups from it.

Settings storage

Everything you change here (groups, names, stars, sounds, watch areas) is saved only in this browser on this device. Other people using this page have their own settings, and nothing here changes what anyone sees in the Wave app.

To use your setup on another laptop, phone or TV, save a personal copy to the camera server under your name with a PIN, then load it on the other device by typing the same name. Each person gets their own copy, and nobody can replace yours without your PIN. Loading copies a setup onto this device only.
